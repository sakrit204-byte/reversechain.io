Methodology

Five quantities, never blended

Most reserve claims fail because different quantities are added together. The proposed methodology keeps five quantities separate. A unit moves from one to the next only when the evidence for that step is approved.

Coverage is defined only as eligible reserve divided by the reserve required for the token supply, both measured in the program's unit. It is calculated only when both values exist and an attestation is current. In every other case the dashboard shows "Not applicable", never a percentage.

Reconciliation

Three ledgers that must agree

Reconciliation compares the on-chain supply, the registry's unit records and the custodian's statement. Any difference blocks new minting until it is resolved.

  1. Supply snapshot

    Issued, Treasury-held, escrowed, burned and redeemed supply is read from the contracts for each program.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  2. Registry snapshot

    Units in custody, eligible and allocated are taken from approved records, down to container and bobbin.

    [rc_status s="in_development"]

  3. Custodian statement

    The custodian's unit list and weights are matched to the registry unit by unit.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  4. Attestation

    An independent attestor reviews the reconciliation. The report hash and an as-of date are posted to ReserveGuard, and the attestation goes stale after the configured window.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

Exceptions and alerts

Problems are published, not smoothed over

The proposed framework treats an exception as reportable information. Open exceptions appear on the dashboard with the date opened and the units affected.

Count mismatch

The custodian's units do not equal the registry's units in custody.

Stale valuation

A valuation has passed its review date, so the eligible reserve is reduced until it is renewed.

Expiring document

A receipt, insurance schedule or certificate is close to expiry or past it.

Stale attestation

The latest attestation is older than the staleness window, so ReserveGuard allows zero minting.
