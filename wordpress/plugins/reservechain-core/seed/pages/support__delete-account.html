Delete your account in the app

Open the ReserveChain app, go to Account → Delete account, enter your password and confirm. Deletion takes effect immediately and signs you out on every device.

What is erased

  • Your name, email address and country of residence
  • Multi-factor authentication secrets and recovery codes
  • Push-notification tokens and in-app notifications
  • The content of your support messages

What may be retained

If identity or compliance checks (KYC/KYB, AML or sanctions screening) were ever recorded on your account, a minimal compliance record without your contact details is retained for the period required by law and then erased. Entries in the tamper-evident audit trail reference only pseudonymous identifiers.

Waitlist registrations are separate from app accounts: use the unsubscribe link in any update email, or ask us below.

Cannot access the app? Send a deletion request with the email address of your account. We will verify the request before acting on it.
