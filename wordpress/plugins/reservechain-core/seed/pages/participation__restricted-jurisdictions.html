Position statement

The EU/EEA position

ReserveChain does not currently intend to offer tokens to residents of, or persons located in, the EU/EEA.

This applies to all 27 EU member states and to Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway. Persons in these countries may read this website and may opt in to general project updates, but they are not intended participants in any future offering. Token issuance remains inactive until formally launched.

Layered controls

Four layers, from website to contract

No single signal decides eligibility. Each layer can block access on its own.

  1. Self-declaration

    Country of residence is required at waitlist registration and account creation. Nationality and current-location fields exist but are activated only where legally required.

    [rc_status s="in_development" label="Active on waitlist"]

  2. Edge location signal

    The country reported by the content-delivery network is recorded alongside the declaration. A mismatch is flagged for review; it does not on its own approve anyone.

    [rc_status s="in_development"]

  3. Verified residence

    KYC or KYB confirms residence or registered office with documents, overriding self-declaration.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  4. On-chain transfer restriction

    The ComplianceRegistry contract holds a blocked-jurisdiction set supplied by configuration. Transfers to or from an address recorded in a blocked jurisdiction, or not approved, would fail. Testnet only.

    [rc_status s="in_development" label="Testnet"]

Blocked access

What a restricted visitor experiences

  • The website remains readable for information purposes.
  • Waitlist registration is recorded as restricted and limited to general updates, only if the visitor opts in.
  • No program documents, eligibility flow or token-acquisition module would be shown.
  • Existing approvals are suspended if residence changes to a restricted jurisdiction.

Participant responsibility

Your obligations, stated plainly

  • Provide accurate residence, location and, where requested, nationality.
  • Do not use VPNs, proxies or other tools to disguise your location.
  • Do not act for, or transfer to, a restricted person.
  • Notify any change of residence promptly.
  • Check that participation is lawful where you are. This website is not legal advice.
