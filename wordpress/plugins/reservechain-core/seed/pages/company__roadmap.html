How this roadmap works

Gates, not deadlines

We do not publish launch dates. Several phases depend on parties we do not control (counsel, laboratories, custodians, insurers, auditors, app-store review), and a date that cannot be kept is a claim that cannot be supported.

Each phase has explicit exit criteria. Phases that activate a sensitive capability (eligibility, token acquisition, issuance, redemption) additionally require written authorization from the project owners and a separate deployment action.
