The market problem

Physical assets, weak digital evidence

  • Specialised industrial materials are documented with certificates that are easily separated from the unit they describe.
  • Ownership, custody and specification records sit in different systems held by different parties.
  • Many tokenization efforts lead with the token and treat the asset evidence as an afterthought.
  • Compliance is often bolted on after issuance rather than enforced at every transfer.

The infrastructure opportunity

Evidence first, token last

  • A registry that treats each lot, container and coil as a distinct record with its own evidence.
  • Claim status on every field, so unverified data can never pass as verified.
  • Issuance mechanically capped by attested reserves.
  • Eligibility enforced on-chain without placing personal data on a blockchain.

Initial programs and scalable model

Two metals to prove the method, a schema to extend it

The first programs are deliberately small, specialised and document-rich. The same record types are designed to extend to further asset classes once each is separately approved.
