Gallery status

A wire you can barely see needs exact photography

At 0.025 mm, a single strand is close to the limit of what the eye can see. Most spool imagery online shows wire tens of times thicker. A ReserveChain gallery image counts as evidence only when it is a real, unedited photograph with capture metadata, a scale reference and a fingerprint, linked to a specific bobbin passport.

The asset owner has not yet supplied and approved any photograph that meets that standard. The frames below show what will be published once photographs are supplied and approved.

Required shot list

Six frames, each tied to a bobbin

Every approved photograph will carry a caption, the capture date, its source, the bobbin passport it documents, its file fingerprint and its usage rights. Until then, every field shows as pending.

Scale accuracy

25 µm, drawn to scale

The wire is roughly a quarter to a third of the diameter of a typical human hair. Any approved image must keep that proportion recognisable. Images that make the wire look like a cable or a thick spooled conductor will be rejected.

Media usage-rights and publication policy

How an image becomes evidence

  1. Original capture supplied

    The unedited original, with capture metadata kept. Derivatives are published only alongside their originals.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  2. Rights and source recorded

    The photographer or source, the licence, permitted uses and any third-party rights, recorded in the media usage-rights field.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  3. Scale and instrument recorded

    The magnification, scale bar and measuring-instrument calibration are recorded for every macro and measurement image.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  4. Fingerprinted and linked

    A SHA-256 fingerprint is computed and the file is linked to the lot passport or a specific bobbin passport.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  5. Reviewed and approved

    Maker-checker review is completed before publication, and every change is logged in the audit trail.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

Concept illustrations are never attached to a passport, registry record or gallery. Where they explain an idea elsewhere, they carry a visible "Concept illustration, not product evidence" label.
