Structural layers

Four layers that must align

The program only works if the physical, legal, compliance and digital layers describe the same thing at the same time. Each layer has its own owner and its own evidence.

L1

Physical asset

Identified lots, containers and coils with Certificates of Analysis, custody records and independent verification.

L2

Legal linkage

[rc_status s="in_development"]

Compliance gate

Jurisdiction, KYC/KYB, AML, sanctions and PEP outcomes mirrored on-chain without personal data.

Digital record

Digital Asset Passports, registry entries and reserve-gated issuance controls on testnet.

Allocation and acquisition logic

How access would be ordered, where approved

Allocation rules are not approved. The table records the design questions the definitive documentation must answer, so that no rule is improvised at launch.

Design questionProposed approachDecision status
Program capacityCapped by attested eligible reserve units and an approved asset-to-token ratio. No capacity exists before attestation.[rc_status s="proposed"]
Per-participant limitsMinimum and maximum participation per approved account, set in configuration.[rc_status s="proposed"]
Ordering of requestsOnly approved accounts may submit; ordering method (e.g. time-stamped or pro-rata) to be fixed in the documents.[rc_status s="proposed"]
Waitlist positionWaitlist registration confers no priority, allocation or entitlement.[rc_status s="not_applicable" label="No priority"]
Early-participation termsAny discount parameter applies only within a published window and cap, under the proposed methodology.[rc_status s="proposed"]

Participant journey

Five gates in summary

The full nine-step journey is described on How Token Acquisition Will Work.

  1. Register interest

    Join the waitlist for updates. Double opt-in. No funds or wallet details.

    [rc_status s="in_development" label="Open · updates only"]

  2. Establish eligibility

    Jurisdiction check, then KYC for individuals or KYB for organisations, with AML, sanctions and PEP screening.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  3. Review evidence and documents

    Digital Asset Passports, Certificates of Analysis, reserve reports and the definitive offering documents.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  4. Request participation

    Through the token-acquisition module, only once it is authorised and activated.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  5. Hold, monitor, redeem

    Portfolio view, reserve information and a proposed physical-redemption route.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

Documentation set

What must exist before launch

  • Definitive offering or program documentation
  • Terms describing what a token does and does not represent
  • Independent verification and valuation reports for each referenced lot
  • Custody agreement and chain-of-custody records
  • Proof of Reserves methodology and first reconciliation
  • Redemption terms, thresholds and fees (where approved)
  • Jurisdiction-specific disclosures and the final restricted list
