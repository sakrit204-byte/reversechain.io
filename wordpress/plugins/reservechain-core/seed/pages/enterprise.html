What enterprise means here

The same infrastructure, for your assets

ReserveChain is being built first for its own copper and nickel programs. The registry, passport, evidence, compliance and issuance-control layers are designed as separable modules, so that asset owners, processors and institutions could later use them for their own physical assets. No enterprise service is live; discussions are welcome.

Module catalogue

Thirteen modules, status shown honestly

"In development" means software exists and is being tested on ReserveChain's own programs. "Proposed" means a defined scope without delivered software.

Onboarding

Structured intake of asset, ownership, location and documentation, with due-diligence checklists.

[rc_status s="in_development"]

Registry

Separate records for program, material, lot, container, coil, laboratory report, valuation and custody location.

[rc_status s="in_development"]

Digital Asset Passports

Per-unit passports with QR access, lifecycle timeline, evidence ledger and Merkle root.

[rc_status s="in_development"]

Verification integrations

Intake of laboratory and inspection results, with SHA-256 fingerprints and field-level claim status.

[rc_status s="proposed"]

Custody integrations

Custody location, chain-of-custody events and release controls linked to registry units.

[rc_status s="proposed"]

Compliance

Jurisdiction rules, KYC/KYB outcome storage and an on-chain eligibility mirror without personal data.

[rc_status s="in_development"]

Tokenization controls

Role-based issuance, pausing and burn, with transfer checks against the compliance registry. Testnet only.

[rc_status s="in_development"]

Reserve engine

Attestations of eligible units that cap issuance; with no approved ratio, issuance is disabled.

[rc_status s="in_development"]

Reporting

Declared-versus-verified reserve views and an exportable, hash-chained audit trail.

[rc_status s="in_development"]

APIs

Read-only public endpoints for programs, passports and document verification; authenticated account endpoints.

[rc_status s="in_development"]

Portals

Enterprise, asset-owner and participant portals, built and kept inactive until authorised.

[rc_status s="in_development" label="Built · inactive"]

Customisation

Asset-class schemas, document types, workflow states and languages configured per client.

[rc_status s="proposed"]

Managed services

Operation of the registry and evidence workflow on a client's behalf, under agreed controls.

[rc_status s="proposed"]

Who it is for

Three enterprise routes

Enterprise Tokenization Services

For organisations that want a governed digital representation of physical inventory, built on ReserveChain's infrastructure.

Technology Licensing & White-Label

For platforms and institutions that want to run the modules under their own brand and governance.

Asset Owners & Originators

For producers and holders who want their material considered for a ReserveChain program.

Industrial Buyers
