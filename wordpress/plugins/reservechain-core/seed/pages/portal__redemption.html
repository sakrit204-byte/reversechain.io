Redemption Portal concept

From a token record to a specific physical unit

This page describes a proposed framework, subject to final legal, contractual, technical and operational confirmation. Physical redemption is not available. No tokens have been issued, so nothing can be redeemed.

The portal is designed around one idea: a redemption request should name the exact container or coil to be released, show every check it must pass, and leave a complete record (burn, release instruction, delivery and title documents) attached to that unit's Digital Asset Passport.

Request lifecycle

Seven stages, each visible to the participant

Proposed workflow. Every stage would show its current state in the portal and in the apps, with maker-checker approval on the operator side.

  1. Redemption request

    Select eligible holdings, program and specific units; accept the applicable terms by e-signature.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  2. Eligibility

    KYC/KYB, sanctions and jurisdiction re-checked at the time of request, not relied on from onboarding.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  3. Verification

    Balance and wallet ownership confirmed; unit availability and packaging constraints checked against the registry.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  4. Settlement and burn

    Tokens locked on approval, then burned; the burn transaction is recorded against the request.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  5. Custody release

    A release instruction issued to the custodian for the named units, with seal numbers checked at release.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  6. Logistics or collection

    Handling assessment, customs and export or import fields, shipment or collection, tracking.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  7. Completion

    Delivery confirmation and title-transfer documents; registry, reserve and supply records updated and reconciled.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

Unit selection

Physical units, not fractions of a pool

Both initial programs are packaged in discrete units, so redemption is proposed at unit level. Minimum units and packaging rules are configurable and subject to approval.

CU 29 · CONTAINER LEVEL

Ultrafine Copper Powder

Owner-supplied records describe glass ampoules packed in cardboard boxes. Redemption is proposed box by box, each box identified by its own passport (for example Box no. 20). Ultrafine powder requires a handling and transport assessment before any release.

[rc_status s="proposed"] View Copper Program →
NI 28 · COIL LEVEL

Nickel Wire 0.025 mm

Owner-supplied records describe 30 bobbins in one box. Redemption is proposed bobbin by bobbin, each with its own passport (for example bobbin 8). Packaging rules for wire of this diameter are to be confirmed with the custodian.

Documents and history

Each completed request would leave these records, fingerprinted and linked to the unit's passport and to the tamper-evident audit trail.

  • Signed request and terms acceptance (version, time, account)
  • Eligibility re-check outcome
  • Token lock and burn record with transaction reference
  • Custody release instruction and seal verification
  • Customs, export and delivery documents
  • Delivery or collection confirmation
  • Title-transfer documentation
  • Registry and reserve reconciliation entry
