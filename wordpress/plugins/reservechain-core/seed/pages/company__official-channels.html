Why this page exists

If it is not listed here, it is not us

Pre-launch projects attract impersonation: fake token sales, fake support agents, look-alike domains and cloned social accounts. This directory is the single reference against which any ReserveChain contact can be checked.

We list only channels that have been confirmed and approved through the four-eyes workflow. Where a channel has not yet been confirmed, we say so rather than leaving a gap that a third party could fill. Any channel, address or account that is not on this page should be treated as unofficial.

ReserveChain is not offering or selling tokens. No ReserveChain channel will ask you to send funds, connect a wallet, share a private key or seed phrase, or pay to reserve an allocation. Anyone who does is not acting for ReserveChain.

Verification steps

Five checks before you act

  1. Check the domain

    Type reservechain.io yourself. Watch for look-alikes: extra letters, hyphens, different endings, or a missing first letter.

    [rc_status s="provided" label="Always"]

  2. Match the channel to this directory

    Compare the exact handle, address or link with the table above. "To be confirmed" means no account is official yet.

    [rc_status s="provided" label="Always"]

  3. Reject any request for funds or keys

    No payment, wallet connection, private key, seed phrase or "reservation fee" is requested by ReserveChain at any stage of pre-launch.

    [rc_status s="provided" label="Always"]

  4. Verify documents by fingerprint

    Hash a document you received on the verification page. If it is not in the registry, it was not published by ReserveChain.

    [rc_status s="provided" label="Always"]

  5. Report it

    Send details of suspected impersonation through the contact form using the "Security disclosure" topic.

    [rc_status s="provided" label="Always"]

How channels are added

Confirmation before publication

A new channel appears in this directory only after the account is controlled by ReserveChain (not by a contractor), recovery and MFA are configured, and the entry has been submitted, independently approved and published.

  • Account registered in ReserveChain's name
  • MFA and recovery under issuer control
  • Entry approved by a second reviewer
  • Change recorded in the audit trail
  • Announced on this domain first
