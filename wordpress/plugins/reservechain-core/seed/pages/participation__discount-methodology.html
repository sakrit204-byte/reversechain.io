2 · Calculation formula

Expressed symbolically, by design

The proposed early-participation acquisition reference A is a fixed fraction of the reference value:

A = R × (1 − d)

where d is the early-participation discount parameter, expressed as a fraction between 0 and 1. The brief proposes d = 0.20; that value is not approved. Consideration for an accepted request would be:

C = q′ × A, where q′ = min(q, l, L − Q)

q = units requested · l = per-participant limit · L = program cap for the early window · Q = units already accepted in that window.

Fixed rules of the formula

Properties that cannot be bent

  • If R cannot be computed, A does not exist and no request can be accepted.
  • d applies only to R; it is never stacked with other incentives.
  • L can never exceed the issuance capacity allowed by attested reserves, E × k.
  • d, l and L are configuration values, unset by default, published only with a written approval reference.
  • Any change to d is recorded in the tamper-evident audit trail.

3 · Rationale

Why a discount might exist: risk compensation

Where an early-participation parameter is approved, its only justification would be that early participants bear risks that later participants may not. It is a recognition of those risks, not a promise of gain, appreciation or any future value.

01

Early-stage risk

Participating before the program has an operating history, with structure, counterparties and processes still being proven.

02

Exit risk

No market, transferability or redemption route may be available for an extended period, or at all.

03

Lock-up

Early terms would likely carry a lock-up or transfer restriction whose length is not yet defined.

04

Execution risk

Custody, reconciliation and redemption depend on arrangements that are not yet in place.

7 · Risk disclosure

What the parameter does not mean

  • A discount to R does not mean a token will ever be worth R, or any amount.
  • R depends on valuations that may change, be revised or prove wrong.
  • The underlying metal may not be realisable at its valuation, or at all.
  • Lock-ups and transfer restrictions may prevent exit when you need it.
  • The parameter may be changed, reduced to zero or withdrawn before launch.
  • Regulatory treatment may differ by jurisdiction and may change.
  • Total loss of the amount committed is possible.
