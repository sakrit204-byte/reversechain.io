The lifecycle

Eight stages. Each one gated by evidence.

A unit moves to the next stage only when the evidence for the current stage exists and has been approved. Stages one to five act on the physical asset and its records. Stages six to eight act on the digital layer and can never run ahead of the physical record.

Stage by stage

What happens, and what it proves

This describes a proposed operating framework, subject to final legal, contractual, technical and operational confirmation. The status at the right of each stage shows how far that stage has been built today.

  1. Identify and source

    The asset owner or originator submits the unit: material, form, lot or batch reference, declared quantity, packaging, origin and the documents they hold. The registry gives it a permanent identifier, for example RC-CU-LOT-000001, before any claim is published.

    [rc_status s="in_development"]

  2. Verify

    Sampling and testing are carried out by an accredited laboratory independent of the owner. The certificate is checked against the issuer, and its document fingerprint is recorded. Until this is done, owner-supplied certificates are shown as owner-supplied.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  3. Value

    A qualified valuer applies a stated methodology and benchmark to the verified specification. The valuation records its date, currency and source, and each valuation carries an expiry.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  4. Custody

    The unit is received into segregated custody, weighed, sealed and recorded down to container or bobbin level. The custodian issues a receipt that links back to the registry identifier.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  5. Digital Asset Passport

    The registry assembles every approved evidence record for the unit into a passport. The passport is sealed with a SHA-256 Merkle root and published only after four-eyes approval.

    [rc_status s="in_development"]

  6. Tokenize

    If the final legal structure permits it, ERC-20 units could be minted against attested reserves only. Minting is blocked by default and limited by the reserve guard.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  7. Reconcile

    Token supply, burned supply, custodian inventory and passport records are compared on a fixed cycle. Any difference opens an exception and blocks further minting.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  8. Redeem

    An eligible holder requests physical delivery. Tokens are escrowed and then burned, and only after that is the specific unit released from custody.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

Gate conditions

What must be true before a unit moves on

Each gate is a condition the software checks. It is not left to judgement in the moment. If a condition is unmet, the unit stays at its current stage and the passport shows why.

  • Into Verify: a registry identifier exists and the owner's KYB and chain-of-title documents are on file.
  • Into Value: an approved verification record links to a certificate whose fingerprint matches the stored file.
  • Into Custody: the custodian's received weight and seal numbers match the verified lot, within tolerance.
  • Into Passport: each stage record is approved by someone other than the person who entered it.
  • Into Tokenize: a current reserve attestation exists, and the guard's ratio and staleness window are configured and approved.
  • Into Redeem: the holder is eligible, the unit is unencumbered, and the tokens are held in escrow.

Participant visibility

Public, participant and restricted

Transparency does not mean everything is published. Record fields carry their own visibility, so commercially sensitive or personal data stays restricted while the evidence that supports a claim remains open to inspection.

Public

  • Passport identifiers and specifications
  • Certificates and their SHA-256 fingerprints
  • Verification, custody and reserve status
  • Proof-of-Reserves summary
  • Audit-trail chain head

Eligible participants (planned)

  • Own holdings and transaction history
  • Redemption requests and their status
  • Program documentation behind the portal
  • Notices relevant to their holdings

Restricted

  • KYC/KYB files and screening results
  • Acquisition cost and commercial terms
  • Exact vault references, where withheld
  • Internal reviewer notes
