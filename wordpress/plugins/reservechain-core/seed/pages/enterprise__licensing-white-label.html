Branding and modes

Ten site modes, one switchboard

The platform runs in defined modes: Development, Pre-Launch, Waitlist, Documentation Release, Asset Verification, Enterprise Onboarding, Eligibility, Early Participation, Live Offering and Redemption. A licensee would control which modules are visible in each mode.

Modes that expose eligibility, acquisition, wallet, payment or redemption functions are locked behind written authorisation and a separate deployment flag. They cannot be turned on from the content editor.

Licensee responsibilities

What stays with the licensee

  • Legal structure, licences and offering documentation in its jurisdictions
  • Selection and contracting of laboratories, custodians and insurers
  • KYC/KYB provider selection and compliance decisions
  • Accuracy of the evidence and data it publishes
  • Control of administrative keys and roles

Licensing model

Configuration, integration and staging deployment.

Usage

Linked to records, passports or programs managed.

Support

Service levels and managed operations where requested.
