Who may register

Open to read, limited to participate

  • Individuals and organisations interested in the initial copper and nickel programs
  • Industrial users, asset owners and enterprise contacts (select "Enterprise services")
  • Adults providing their own details, with their own email address

If you are resident in, or located in, the EU/EEA or another restricted jurisdiction, your registration is recorded as restricted. You may opt in to general project updates only, with no offering-related communications.

Waitlist

Does joining give me priority or a better price?

No. The waitlist confers no priority, allocation, price or entitlement. Any future terms will be set out only in definitive documentation.

Why do you ask for my country?

To apply jurisdiction rules from the start. ReserveChain does not currently intend to offer tokens to residents of, or persons located in, the EU/EEA.

What is a consent fingerprint?

A SHA-256 hash of the exact notice text you acknowledged. If the notice changes, the fingerprint changes, so your record shows precisely what you agreed to.

How do I leave the waitlist or delete my data?

Ask us through the contact page to unsubscribe, correct or delete your registration; requests are handled under the retention procedures. See the Privacy Policy for your rights.

Someone offered me a waitlist "allocation" for payment. Is that real?

No. It is fraudulent. Do not pay and report it through the Anti-Fraud Notice.
