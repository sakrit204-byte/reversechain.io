Scope of this page

Redemption is the test of the whole chain

This page describes a proposed physical-redemption framework, subject to final legal, contractual, technical and operational confirmation. No tokens have been issued, so nothing can be redeemed. The redemption module and its contract are built but disabled.

Every earlier link exists so that this one can work. A redemption names a specific unit. That unit has a passport, the passport has evidence, and the custodian holds the unit under a receipt.

The process

Seven stages, one irreversible order

The sequence is fixed: escrow, then approval, then burn, then release. Metal never leaves custody against tokens that still exist, and tokens are never burned for a unit that has not been confirmed.

  1. Redemption request

    The holder opens a request in the portal. They select the program and either name the specific container or bobbin or let the system allocate one, and they choose delivery or collection. Their tokens move into escrow in RedemptionManager.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  2. Eligibility

    Compliance confirms that the holder's KYC/KYB is current, that the jurisdiction is permitted for physical delivery, that sanctions screening is clear, and that the request meets the program's thresholds once they are set.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  3. Verification

    The selected unit is confirmed against its passport: seal intact, weight within tolerance, unencumbered and not allocated to another request. A failed check reopens the selection.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  4. Settlement and burn

    An operator approves the request. The escrowed tokens are burned on-chain and a fulfilment reference is recorded. Applicable fees are settled under the approved terms.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  5. Custody release

    The custodian receives a dual-approved release order that cites the burn transaction, then releases the unit with its seal and documentation.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  6. Logistics or collection

    The unit is packed to the material's handling requirements, with customs and transport documents where needed. It is delivered or collected against identity confirmation.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  7. Completion

    Receipt is confirmed. The passport moves to "Released" with its full history, and reserve and supply figures update after reconciliation.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

Exits from the process

What happens when a request stops

A redemption framework is judged by its failure paths as much as by its main path. Each one ends with the tokens or the unit in a known state.

Unit-level redemption

A specific box. A specific bobbin.

The redemption unit is set by the material's form, so that a holder receives a documented, sealed and traceable item rather than an anonymous quantity.

Cu 29

Copper powder: container-level

Powder sealed in glass ampoules and packed in cardboard boxes is redeemed by the container, which keeps the seal and the passport intact. Splitting ampoules out of a box is not proposed. The quantity per container is pending documentation.

Ni 28

Nickel wire: bobbin-level

Wire 0.025 mm in diameter cannot be cut and re-spooled without risk to its condition, so it is redeemed by the bobbin. Lot 120/NP1 comprises 30 bobbins, each with its own passport. Net weight per bobbin is pending.
