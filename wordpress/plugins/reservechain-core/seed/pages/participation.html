Purpose

Why an early program is being designed

The intent is to let a small, fully screened group engage with the first asset programs while the evidence base, controls and documentation are tested at limited scale.

Test the chain of trust at small scale

Asset identity, verification, custody records, reserve reconciliation and transfer controls should operate end-to-end on a limited cohort before any wider availability is considered.

Align early participants with evidence

Participants would review Digital Asset Passports, Certificates of Analysis and definitive documents before any step is possible. Evidence comes first; access follows.

Recognise early-stage risk explicitly

Any early-participation terms, including a proposed discount parameter, would exist to reflect the additional risks of an early stage, not as an incentive or promise. See the proposed methodology.

Intended participant profile

Who the program is designed for

The framework assumes participants who understand early-stage, non-transferable and documentation-dependent arrangements, and who can complete full identity and source-of-funds checks.

  • Individuals and organisations resident in jurisdictions where participation is legally permitted
  • Participants able to pass KYC or KYB, AML, sanctions and PEP screening
  • Industrial users and organisations with a genuine interest in copper powder or nickel wire programs
  • Participants who may be classified as professional, institutional or otherwise qualified where the final legal structure requires it

Excluded by design

Who it is not for

  • Residents of, or persons located in, the EU/EEA. ReserveChain does not currently intend to offer tokens to them.
  • Persons in restricted or sanctioned jurisdictions, or on sanctions lists
  • Anyone acting as a nominee or intermediary for an ineligible person
  • Anyone seeking short-term gains, assured outcomes or immediate exit options

Final eligibility criteria are subject to legal review. See Eligibility & KYC and Restricted Jurisdictions.

Key risks

Read these before registering interest

  • The program may never launch, or may launch in a materially different form.
  • Asset specifications, quantities and values are not yet independently verified.
  • Custody, insurance and redemption arrangements are not in place.
  • Any future token may have no market, no transferability and no assured exit.
  • Regulatory classification and jurisdictional treatment are not yet determined.
  • Smart contracts, wallets and infrastructure carry technical risk. See Risk Disclosure.
