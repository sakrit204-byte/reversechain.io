Who this is for

Material with evidence, owners with patience

ReserveChain is looking for physical assets whose identity, specification, ownership and location can be proven document by document. Submitting an asset starts a due-diligence process. It is not an acceptance, a valuation or a commitment by either side.

01

Producers and refiners

Manufacturers of high-purity metals, powders, wires and other specified industrial materials.

02

Owners and holders

Companies and institutions holding identifiable inventory with clear title.

03

Originators and suppliers

Parties sourcing material on behalf of owners, with documented authority to act.

Due-diligence workflow

Eight stages from submission to program

Each stage produces evidence that is fingerprinted and linked to the asset's registry record. A failure at any stage stops the process.

  1. Submission

    General description, location, quantity range and available documents, through the contact form.

    [rc_status s="in_development" label="Open"]

  2. Counterparty due diligence

    KYB on the submitting entity and its beneficial owners; sanctions and PEP screening.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  3. Title and encumbrance review

    Proof of ownership, chain of title and confirmation of no liens or competing claims.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  4. Independent verification

    Inspection, sampling and laboratory testing by independent parties, compared with supplied certificates.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  5. Valuation

    Independent valuation using a documented methodology suitable for the material.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  6. Custody

    Transfer to, or confirmation at, an approved custody location with chain-of-custody records.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  7. Registry and passport creation

    Unit-level records (lot, container, coil) and Digital Asset Passports with evidence ledger.

    [rc_status s="in_development"]

  8. Reserve controls and tokenization path

    Attestation of eligible units; any tokenization only under approved legal terms.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

Document checklist

What to have ready before submitting

CategoryDocumentsWhy it matters
Identity of materialCertificates of Analysis, specifications, lot or batch identifiers, packaging descriptionEstablishes what the asset is and links documents to physical units
OwnershipPurchase or production records, invoices, title documents, encumbrance statementsEstablishes who may lawfully deal with the asset
Location and conditionWarehouse receipts, storage conditions, photographs of packaging and labelsEstablishes where it is and whether it can be inspected
CounterpartyCorporate registry extract, beneficial-ownership declaration, signatory authorityRequired for KYB and sanctions screening
LogisticsExport classification, transport and handling requirementsDetermines custody and any future redemption logistics

What ReserveChain will not do

Boundaries for every submission

  • Publish your data without your approval and our four-eyes review
  • Present owner-supplied figures as verified
  • Indicate a value, price or token amount before independent valuation and approval
  • Name you as a partner without written authorisation
