The proposed ReserveChain model

Evidence first. Tokens only if approved.

ReserveChain is designing one coherent chain of trust between a physical lot of industrial metal and any future digital representation of it. Every claim on this platform carries a status, every document carries a fingerprint, and nothing is shown as confirmed until the evidence behind it is approved.

Physical

Documented material

Ultra-high-purity copper powder and high-purity nickel wire, identified down to the container and the individual bobbin.

Evidence

Fingerprinted documents

Certificates, custody and ownership records are hashed with SHA-256 on upload; anyone can check a file against the registry.

Identity

Digital Asset Passports

Each lot, container and coil receives a passport assembled from evidence, never typed by hand, with a Merkle root over its documents.

Tokenization

Planned ERC-20 layer

Testnet smart contracts in which minting is fail-closed and bound to attested reserves. No token exists; nothing is offered.

How the proposed model will work

From physical lot to digital record

The proposed industrial-metals tokenization workflow. Each stage produces evidence; the next stage cannot begin until that evidence is reviewed under the four-eyes principle.

  1. Asset Program Selection

    A defined industrial-metal program is selected with its product specification, unit structure (lot, container, coil) and documentation requirements.

    [rc_status s="in_development"]

  2. Independent Material Verification

    Accredited laboratories test sampled material against the declared specification; certificates are fingerprinted and linked to the exact physical units sampled.

    [rc_status s="pending_verification"]

  3. Ownership and Custody Documentation

    Title, chain-of-title and custody records are collected and reviewed before any asset can be considered for a reserve.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  4. Digital Asset Passport

    Each approved unit receives a persistent passport combining specification, evidence, custody and status history, with QR access and machine-readable export.

    [rc_status s="in_development"]

  5. Tokenization

    ERC-20 tokens or asset-program token series may be issued under the final legal and corporate structure, only against attested reserves and only after written authorization.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  6. Administration and Reserve Reconciliation

    Token supply, physical inventory and custody records are reconciled; discrepancies surface as exceptions rather than being smoothed away.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  7. Transfer and Redemption

    Eligible holders may, under the final terms, request physical redemption by container or coil, with tokens burned on release.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

Proposed independent verification framework

Trust is built before an asset is tokenized

This describes a proposed framework subject to final legal, contractual, technical and operational confirmation.

  • Sampling by the laboratory itself, with the sampled container or bobbin recorded
  • Accredited methods (e.g. ISO/IEC 17025), no laboratory has yet been appointed by ReserveChain
  • Certificates distinguished by provenance: owner-supplied, laboratory-direct, independently verified
  • Re-verification on custody transfer, ageing or any discrepancy

Proposed ERC-20 tokenization architecture

Contracts that fail closed

A testnet suite with automated tests. Nothing is deployed to Ethereum mainnet, no contract address is published and every tokenomics parameter is deliberately unset pending written approval.

ReserveToken

Controlled issuance

Configurable name, symbol and cap. Minting is blocked unless backed by the reserve guard or an explicitly approved cap. Pausable, role-based, multisig-ready.

ReserveGuard

Mint ≤ attested reserve

Attestors post reserve reports by hash; the mintable amount is derived from them and expires when an attestation goes stale.

ComplianceRegistry

Transfer restrictions

Eligibility status, jurisdiction codes, expiry and freeze flags checked on every transfer, with EU/EEA codes blockable by configuration.

RedemptionManager

Escrow, then burn

Redemption requests escrow tokens, pass compliance review and burn only on approved physical release. Disabled by default.

Treasury

Limits & timelock

Daily withdrawal limits and an optional timelock queue for large movements, administered by role.

AuditAnchor

Audit trail on-chain

Periodically commits the head of the platform's hash-chained audit log, so even a full database rewrite would be detectable.

Proposed physical-redemption process

From digital position to physical delivery

This describes a proposed framework subject to final legal, contractual, technical and operational confirmation. Redemption is not available.

  1. Redemption request

    An eligible holder selects a program and, where permitted, specific containers or coils.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  2. Eligibility & compliance re-check

    KYC/KYB, sanctions and jurisdiction are re-screened at the time of request.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  3. Unit verification

    The selected units' passports, seals and custody records are reconciled before release.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  4. Settlement and burn

    Escrowed tokens are burned on-chain; the burn transaction is written to the unit's passport.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  5. Custody release, logistics and delivery

    Release under dual control, customs documentation and delivery confirmation. Fees and thresholds to be determined.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

Risks and limitations

Understand the risks before participating

Development stage

The corporate and legal structure, custody, insurance, valuation and token terms are not final and may change or not proceed.

Evidence risk

Current certificates are owner-supplied and not yet independently verified. Declared weights come from the customer, not the laboratory.

Market & liquidity

Industrial-metal values move; no liquidity, price, return or appreciation is promised or implied.

Regulatory

Token classification and permitted jurisdictions are subject to legal review; EU/EEA residents are not intended participants.

Technology

Smart contracts, wallets and blockchains carry technical risk. Contracts are unaudited and testnet-only.

Redemption & logistics

Any future physical redemption depends on custody, logistics, customs and fees that are yet to be determined.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Can I buy ReserveChain tokens today?

No. ReserveChain is in development. No tokens exist, none are offered or sold, and registering interest creates no allocation, reservation or entitlement.

Are the copper and nickel materials verified?

The programs are documented by owner-supplied Certificates of Analysis, which are fingerprinted and published with an exact transcription. They have not yet been independently verified by ReserveChain; their status is shown as pending verification.

Where are the metals stored and are they insured?

No custodian, warehouse or insurer has been appointed. Custody and insurance are proposed arrangements, subject to final approval.

What is a Digital Asset Passport?

A public, evidence-derived record for a lot, container or coil: specification, certificates, custody and status history, a Merkle root over its documents, a QR code and a JSON export.

Can residents of the EU or EEA participate?

ReserveChain does not currently intend to offer tokens to residents of, or persons located in, the EU/EEA.

How do I know a document really comes from ReserveChain?

Check its SHA-256 fingerprint on the Verification page. The check runs in your browser; the file is never uploaded.
