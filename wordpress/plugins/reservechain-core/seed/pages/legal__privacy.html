1. Controller and scope

Who is responsible for your data

This policy covers personal data processed through reservechain.io, its forms, its public registry and, once they are activated, its portals. The data controller will be the ReserveChain operating entity. That entity is to be confirmed upon incorporation, because the Swiss corporate structure is still in development. Until then, privacy requests are handled through the contact channel in section 13. This policy will be updated, and the update announced, once the controller is established.

The policy is written with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) in mind, and with the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation where those apply. Where the GDPR applies, a representative in the EU will be appointed if one is required.

5–8. Recipients, transfers, retention and security

Where your data goes and how it is protected

5. Processors and recipients

Personal data is shared only with service providers acting on ReserveChain's instructions under written data-processing terms, and only to the extent each one needs. Processors will be named in this policy once appointed. They fall into these categories:

  • website hosting, content delivery and network security (including the edge service that supplies an indicative country signal);
  • transactional email delivery, for confirmation and reply messages;
  • backup and infrastructure monitoring;
  • a KYC/KYB and sanctions-screening provider, in future and only if participation is activated;
  • professional advisers bound by confidentiality, and authorities where the law requires disclosure.

ReserveChain does not sell personal data, does not share it for third-party advertising, and does not put personal data on any blockchain.

6. International transfers

Some processors may hold data outside Switzerland or the EEA. Transfers will go only to countries with an adequate level of protection recognised by Switzerland or the EU. Otherwise they will be covered by appropriate safeguards, such as the standard contractual clauses recognised by the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) and the European Commission. You can request details of the safeguards used.

7. Retention

Final retention periods are to be defined by counsel under applicable law. The intended principles are:

  • an unconfirmed waitlist registration is deleted if the double opt-in is not completed;
  • waitlist data is kept until you withdraw consent or the waitlist closes. The record of the disclosure you accepted may be kept longer, as evidence;
  • contact enquiries are kept for as long as is needed to deal with them and any follow-up;
  • KYC/KYB records, if collected in future, are kept for the periods AML law requires;
  • audit-trail entries are append-only and contain hashed identifiers, not plain IP addresses. They are kept to preserve integrity of the record.

8. Security

Measures include TLS encryption in transit, restricted role-based access to personal data, and two-person approval for content and registry changes. Further measures:

  • Consent fingerprinting: the exact disclosure text you accepted is stored as a SHA-256 fingerprint with its version, so it can be shown later without ambiguity.
  • Hashed IP addresses: the audit trail and rate-limit controls store a keyed hash (HMAC-SHA-256) of the IP address, never the address itself.
  • Hashed confirmation tokens: double opt-in links are checked against a stored hash, not the token itself.
  • Data minimisation on-chain: on-chain eligibility records hold status flags only.

No system is completely secure. If a personal-data breach is likely to result in a high risk to you, it will be notified to the competent authority and, where required, to you.

9–11. Cookies, rights and marketing

Your choices and your rights

9. Cookies

By default only essential cookies are set: the language preference, and login cookies for authorised staff. No analytics or marketing cookies are set before you consent. See the Cookie Policy.

10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can withdraw consent at any time, which does not affect earlier processing. You may complain to the FDPIC or, where the GDPR applies, to your local supervisory authority.

11. Marketing

We send updates only to waitlist registrants who confirmed by double opt-in. Every update will include an unsubscribe link. Persons in restricted jurisdictions receive general project updates only, if they have opted in. We do not profile you for marketing.

12–14. Automated decisions, contact and changes

12. Automated decision-making

The waitlist applies an automatic jurisdiction status based on the country you declare. For example, EU/EEA registrations are recorded as restricted. This affects only which updates you receive. It has no legal or similarly significant effect. Any future eligibility decision would include human review.

13. Contact

Send privacy requests through the contact form with the subject "Privacy". We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request. We will never ask for a password, seed phrase or private key.

14. Changes to this policy

Each version is registered with a version number and a SHA-256 fingerprint in the Documentation Library. Material changes will be announced in News & Announcements and, where required, notified to waitlist registrants.
