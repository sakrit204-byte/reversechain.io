Warning

Never trust an address from any other source

Scammers impersonate new projects by publishing look-alike token contracts and "official" addresses. They do this on social media, in messaging groups, through paid search results, on token-listing sites and in direct messages. These addresses often look plausible and are sometimes promoted by accounts that copy ReserveChain's name and logo.

  • An address is official only if it appears on this page on reservechain.io, reached by typing the domain yourself.
  • ReserveChain will never send you an address in a direct message, email attachment or chat group.
  • Do not approve token allowances, sign messages or send funds to any address claiming to be ReserveChain.
  • Block-explorer labels, token-listing pages and aggregator entries are not official sources.

If you were given a ReserveChain address elsewhere, do not interact with it. Report it through the Anti-Fraud Notice. Include the address, where you saw it and any screenshots.

Publication protocol

How addresses will be published

When any deployment is authorised, testnet or otherwise, its addresses will be published through three channels that confirm one another. All three must agree.

  1. This page

    Addresses are entered in the CMS and approved under four-eyes review. They then appear in the panel below, with network, contract name, deployment transaction and block-explorer link.

    [rc_status s="in_development"]

  2. Signed announcement

    A dated announcement in News & Announcements lists the same addresses. It will be signed by a key whose public identity is published in the Official Channels Directory.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  3. Audit-trail anchoring

    The publication event is written to the hash-chained audit trail. The trail head is intended to be anchored on-chain through the AuditAnchor contract, which fixes when and what was published.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  4. Changes only by new announcement

    Addresses are never silently edited. Any replacement, migration or pause is announced through the same three channels. The superseded address stays listed with its status.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

Checking an address

Four checks before you rely on one

Domain

You reached this page by typing reservechain.io, not through a link someone sent you.

Character match

Compare every character of the address, not only the first and last few. Look-alike addresses are designed to defeat quick checks.

Network

Confirm the network. A testnet address is not a mainnet address, and testnet tokens have no value.

Announcement

Confirm that the same address appears in the dated, signed announcement in News & Announcements.
