Purpose of this page

Structure first, explained before it is final

Swiss corporate and issuance structure in development. This page sets out the roles we expect a final structure to require and the questions counsel must answer, so that readers can see how the pieces are intended to relate.

It does not describe an incorporated entity, an executed agreement or an appointed counterparty. No legal rights in any industrial metal are created or represented by anything on this website. Every role, relationship and option below remains subject to final legal review, contractual documentation and approval.

Roles under evaluation

Who is expected to do what

Separating these roles is the point of the structure: the party that records the asset should not be the party that holds it, values it or certifies it.

ROLE 01

Issuer

The entity that would issue any token or token series under definitive offering documentation. Expected to sit within the Swiss corporate and issuance structure now in development.

[rc_status s="in_development"]
ROLE 02

Asset-holding vehicle

A segregated vehicle intended to hold title to program material, ring-fenced from operating activity. Whether it is separate from the issuer, and in which jurisdiction, is under evaluation.

[rc_status s="proposed"]
ROLE 03

Custodian or warehouse

An independent operator for sealed intake, segregated storage, inventory records, inspections and release instructions. Location and operator to be confirmed.

ROLE 04

Trustee or collateral agent

An option under evaluation: an independent party holding security or rights over the reserve for the benefit of token holders, acting on defined instructions.

Laboratory, inspector and valuer

Independent parties producing assay, inspection and valuation evidence. Their reports would be fingerprinted and attached to each Digital Asset Passport.

Open legal questions

What must be answered before any issuance

Until these questions are answered in writing by counsel and reflected in definitive documentation, token issuance stays inactive and no page describes tokens as conferring rights.

  • What right, if any, a token would represent in the underlying material.
  • How title passes on physical redemption, and at which point.
  • How the reserve ranks if any party in the structure became insolvent.
  • Regulatory characterisation of the token in each permitted jurisdiction.
  • Governing law and dispute resolution for each agreement.
  • Jurisdiction of custody, export and import constraints for the material.
  • Eligibility rules, including the exclusion of EU/EEA residents and persons located there.
