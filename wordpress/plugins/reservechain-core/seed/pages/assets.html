Two documented programs. Nothing offered.

Future categories

Outside the totals by design

The platform is designed to carry further asset classes. None has an asset, partner or approval behind it. They appear only so the long-term scope is clear, and every card is badged as pre-launch.

Future categories in detail

Admission

How an asset enters the catalogue

  1. Submission

    The asset owner or originator submits the material, its documents and its identifiers.

    [rc_status s="in_development"]

  2. Due diligence and acceptance

    Ownership, laboratory and packaging records are reviewed. The status becomes accepted, rejected, pending review or remediation.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  3. Registry and passport creation

    Program, lot and unit records are created, and each document is fingerprinted.

    [rc_status s="in_development"]

  4. Catalogue listing

    The entry is published with live status facets. It is never marked available until it has been authorised.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]
