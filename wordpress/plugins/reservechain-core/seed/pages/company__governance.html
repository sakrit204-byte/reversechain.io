Platform governance

Four eyes on every record, bound to its fingerprint

Pages, asset records, certificates, passports and documents follow one workflow. Drafts and items under review are never public, indexed or exposed through the API, and published records are unpublished or archived rather than deleted.

Approval bound to content

Approval stores a SHA-256 fingerprint of the content and registry fields. Any later change, however small, invalidates approval and returns the item to review automatically.

No back doors

Direct publishing through the editor's publish button, the REST API or quick edit is intercepted and routed into review. Editors do not hold a publish capability.

Every transition recorded

Each step is appended to the item's history and to the tamper-evident audit trail, with user, time, action, previous and new state and the fingerprint.

Role separation

Five roles, no single point of approval

Staff accounts require multi-factor authentication. Role changes, logins, failed logins and setting changes are themselves written to the audit trail.

Asset governance

A status is a claim. Claims need evidence.

Ownership, laboratory, custody, insurance and reserve claims remain unpublished until the supporting evidence has been supplied and approved. Every value in the registry carries a data-source reference; values without a source stay empty.

View Asset Registry →

  • Owner-supplied data is labelled as owner-supplied and subject to documentary verification.
  • Certificates are transcribed exactly and the original scan is fingerprinted and published alongside.
  • Status values come from restricted lists; confirmation states are intended to require attached evidence and a second approver.
  • Reconciliation is intended to detect and report inconsistencies, never to adjust them silently.
  • Future asset categories are never mixed into current program records or totals.
  • Illustrative content is always marked as illustrative or demo data.

Conflicts management

Disclose, separate, record

  • Relationships between ReserveChain and asset owners or originators are to be disclosed in program documentation.
  • Independent verification, valuation and custody are intended to be performed by parties unrelated to the asset owner.
  • Any commercial spread between acquisition cost, assessed value and introduction value is to be disclosed under approved methodology.
  • A conflicts register is proposed, maintained under the same audit trail.

Tamper-evident audit trail

History that cannot be quietly rewritten

Every workflow transition, document upload, registry change, login and setting change is appended to a single log. Four independent layers make any alteration detectable.

  1. Application

    No code path updates or deletes audit rows, and there is no admin screen to do so.

    [rc_status s="provided" label="Active"]

  2. Database triggers

    BEFORE UPDATE and BEFORE DELETE triggers reject any change to the table, even from a direct database connection.

    [rc_status s="provided" label="Active"]

  3. Hash chain

    Each row stores the SHA-256 hash of the previous row. Editing, inserting or removing any entry breaks every later hash, and the full-chain check reports it.

    [rc_status s="provided" label="Active"]

  4. On-chain anchoring

    The chain head (sequence number and hash) can be written to a public blockchain through the AuditAnchor contract, so even a complete database rewrite would be detectable.

    [rc_status s="in_development" label="Testnet"]
