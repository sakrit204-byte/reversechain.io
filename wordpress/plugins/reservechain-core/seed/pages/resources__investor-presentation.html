Thesis

Representations of physical metal need unit-level evidence

Digital representations of physical assets usually stand on aggregate claims, which outsiders cannot easily test. ReserveChain's thesis is that trust should be built from the bottom up. Each physical unit is identified, its evidence is fingerprinted, its status is shown field by field, and any representation is bounded by independently attested reserves.

Specialised materials, specific evidence

Ultra-pure powders and ultrafine wire are defined by assay, lot and packaging, not by a generic grade. Their documentation suits a registry that tracks units rather than a pooled balance.

Controls built into the contracts

Eligibility checks on every transfer and reserve-gated minting are written into the contracts. They are not left to policy alone, and the default state is the most restrictive one.

Reusable infrastructure

The registry, passports, verification tooling and contract suite are built to serve asset classes beyond the first two programs, subject to approval.

Platform modules and business model

What is built, and how it could be offered

The business model is described only in qualitative terms. Pricing, fees and commercial terms have not been set. Nothing here indicates revenue or a financial outcome.

Modules

  • Industrial-metals registry, with a program, lot, container and coil hierarchy
  • Public Digital Asset Passports with field-level claim status
  • Client-side SHA-256 document verification
  • Hash-chained, tamper-evident audit trail
  • Compliance registry, reserve guard, redemption manager and treasury contracts (test networks only)
  • Participant, enterprise and asset-owner portals, built but inactive

Intended service lines (qualitative)

  • Asset programs: onboarding, registration and evidence management for approved industrial-metal programs.
  • Enterprise tokenization services: configuring the framework for third-party asset owners.
  • Technology licensing and white-label: licensing individual modules to other platforms.
  • Industrial supply interface: giving qualified industrial users access to documented, unit-level material records.

All service lines are proposed and depend on the final legal structure and approvals.
