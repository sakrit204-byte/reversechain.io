Unit of control

Container versus coil

How a metal is packed decides how it is controlled. The registry models both cases natively, so reconciliation and redemption always work on whole physical units.

Sector context

Where specialty metals are used

This is general context on why traceable high-purity metals matter to industry. It does not describe any customer or commitment.

Electronics

Trace impurities affect conductivity and failure rates, so purity documentation is part of qualification.

Energy systems

Battery and storage research relies on consistent, documented feedstock.

Advanced manufacturing

Powder and wire processes need physical specifications, not just chemistry.

Research and medical

Laboratories and device makers need full material traceability for each unit.
