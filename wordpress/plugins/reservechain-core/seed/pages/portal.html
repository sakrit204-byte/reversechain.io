Portal concept

One account view, every status visible

The portal is designed so that a participant sees the same evidence the public registry shows, plus their own account, eligibility and documents, with each module clearly marked active or inactive.

The same functionality is being built into the ReserveChain iOS and Android apps, which share the portal's backend, authentication, permissions and compliance status. The apps are in development; TestFlight and Google Play testing are pending.

Nothing in the portal enables a purchase, a payment or a wallet connection during pre-launch. No tokens exist, so no holdings exist.

Security settings

Account protection by design

Multi-factor authentication

Time-based one-time codes for every account; enforced for all staff roles. Codes cannot be reused within their window.

Sessions and devices

Session expiry and refresh tokens that rotate on every use; reuse of an old token revokes all sessions. App credentials are held in the device's protected storage.

Wallet ownership proof (future)

If wallet functions are ever authorized, a wallet would need signature-based ownership proof and risk screening before use.
