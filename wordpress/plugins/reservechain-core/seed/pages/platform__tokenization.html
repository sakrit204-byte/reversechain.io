Scope of this page

The last link, built to fail closed

This page describes a proposed tokenization framework, subject to final legal, contractual, technical and operational confirmation. ERC-20 tokens or asset-program token series may be issued under the final legal and corporate structure. That structure is in development, and token issuance is inactive.

No token exists. There is no token symbol, price, supply, ratio or contract address, and no secondary market is provided or implied. ReserveChain does not currently intend to offer tokens to residents of, or persons located in, the EU/EEA.

Preconditions

Before a single unit could be minted

Tokenization comes last because it depends on every link before it. The conditions below are cumulative, and each one is checked by a separate party or contract.

  1. Asset onboarding

    The owner has passed KYB, the chain-of-title documents are approved and the unit has a registry identifier.

    [rc_status s="in_development"]

  2. Asset eligibility

    The unit is independently verified, valued, held in custody under receipt, unencumbered and accepted into the program.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  3. Asset identity

    An approved Digital Asset Passport with a current Merkle root exists for every unit to be allocated.

    [rc_status s="in_development"]

  4. Legal and operational linkage

    Final offering documentation defines what a token represents, how it relates to the units in custody and under which law.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  5. Reserve attestation

    An independent attestor's report hash and as-of date are posted to ReserveGuard, inside the staleness window.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  6. Authorization

    The program's parameters are approved, and the issuance module is activated with a written authorization reference that is recorded in the audit trail.

    [rc_status s="not_applicable" label="Inactive"]

Issuance controls

Minting is blocked unless proven otherwise

ReserveToken refuses to mint unless one of two explicitly configured modes applies. If configuration is missing, nothing can be minted. A key holding the minter role can never mint an unlimited amount.

A

Reserve mode (intended)

Every mint asks ReserveGuard for the maximum mintable amount: attested units × tokens-per-unit − current supply. The result is zero if the ratio is unset, the staleness window is unset or the latest attestation is out of date.

B

Static-cap mode (restricted)

This mode applies only when the guard is disabled, a non-zero cap is set and minting has been explicitly enabled by the administrator. Supply can never exceed the cap. It is intended for testing, not for reserve-linked issuance.

Token lifecycle and transfer rules

Every transfer checked against eligibility

If tokens are ever issued, each transfer would be screened by the ComplianceRegistry. A transfer goes through only if both sender and recipient are eligible. A mint screens the recipient, and a burn screens the sender.

Approved

The address holds an approved KYC/KYB decision, mirrored on-chain by a separately permissioned operator.

Not frozen

Compliance can freeze an individual address without pausing the whole program.

Not expired

Each approval carries an expiry, so screening that has lapsed stops transfers.

Permitted jurisdiction

Each address carries a jurisdiction code. Codes in the blocked set, which is seeded with EU/EEA codes, are refused.

  1. Issue

    Mint to an eligible recipient, within the reserve ceiling, after attestation.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  2. Hold

    The token is held in an eligible wallet or in the role-controlled Treasury, which has daily limits and a timelock queue.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  3. Transfer

    Transfers are permitted only between eligible addresses, and only when the final legal structure allows them.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  4. Reconcile

    Supply is compared with eligible reserves and custodian statements on every change. An exception blocks further mints.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  5. Retire or burn

    Tokens are burned on physical redemption, or retired if a unit leaves the program. The burned supply is published.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

Token-program parameters

Configurable, never hard-coded

Supply, the token-to-kilogram ratio, the token-to-container or token-to-bobbin ratio, redemption thresholds and allocations are registry fields that move through Pending, Under Review, Approved and Published. Participation

Built, and deliberately locked

The token-acquisition module exists so that its controls can be reviewed before anything is activated. It cannot accept funds, connect wallets or reserve tokens. Activation would require the final legal structure, definitive offering documentation, eligibility controls and written authorization.
