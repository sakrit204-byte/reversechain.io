The procurement problem

Purity claims are easy. Proof travels badly.

Ultra-high-purity materials are specified to parts per million, yet the paperwork that proves a specification is often a scanned certificate detached from the unit it describes. ReserveChain links each lot, container and coil to its certificate, its fingerprint and its custody history. No material is currently offered for sale; this page is for enquiries.

Proposed procurement workflow

Six steps, none active yet

  1. Enquiry

    Material, specification, form, approximate quantity range, destination and timing.

    [rc_status s="in_development" label="Open"]

  2. Counterparty checks

    KYB, sanctions and end-use screening appropriate to the material and destination.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  3. Specification match

    Comparison of your requirement with passport evidence; samples or independent testing where agreed.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  4. Commercial terms

    Agreed directly in a written contract. No price is quoted on this website.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  5. Release from custody

    Custody release recorded against the unit's passport.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  6. Delivery or collection

    Packaging, transport, export documentation and handover, with the passport following the unit.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

Physical delivery and redemption

Two routes to the same metal

An industrial user could obtain material through direct procurement, or, if a token program is ever launched, a holder could request physical redemption. Both routes would draw on the same registry units, so a unit released for one cannot be counted for the other.

Redemption is a proposed framework subject to final legal, contractual, technical and operational confirmation. Thresholds, fees and logistics are not defined.
