A described process, not an open one

This page explains how the token-acquisition module is designed to work if, and only if, it is authorised. Token issuance remains inactive until formally launched. No step below can be completed today except registering interest and creating a basic account, neither of which confers any right.

Step by step

Nine steps, each with a status

The status at the right of each step reflects what exists today. A step marked proposed is a design, not a service.

  1. Create account

    A participant account with email verification and optional TOTP multi-factor authentication. The account itself gives access to notices and status only, and confers no right to participate.

    [rc_status s="in_development" label="Built · limited"]

  2. Eligibility

    Country of residence is checked against the EU/EEA restriction and the configurable restricted list. An edge-network location signal is recorded to flag mismatches. Restricted accounts stop here.

    [rc_status s="proposed" label="Planned"]

  3. KYC / KYB

    Identity verification for individuals, or business verification with beneficial-ownership checks for organisations, plus AML, sanctions and PEP screening by an external provider (not yet selected).

    [rc_status s="proposed" label="Planned"]

  4. Review available program

    Approved accounts would see only programs that have passed verification, custody and reserve attestation, with their Digital Asset Passports and reserve status.

    [rc_status s="proposed" label="Planned"]

  5. Review documents

    Definitive offering documentation, terms, risk disclosures and jurisdiction-specific notices, each with a SHA-256 fingerprint. Acknowledgement would be recorded against the exact document version.

    [rc_status s="proposed" label="Planned"]

  6. Acquisition / settlement

    A request through the token-acquisition module within published limits. Settlement method, timing and any early-participation parameter are not approved. No payment can be made through this website.

    [rc_status s="proposed" label="Planned · locked"]

  7. Account / wallet

    Tokens, if issued, would be delivered only to an address registered as approved in the ComplianceRegistry contract. Wallet connection is not available in pre-launch.

    [rc_status s="proposed" label="Planned · locked"]

  8. Portfolio

    Holdings, transaction history, linked passports, reserve information and notices in the participant portal.

    [rc_status s="proposed" label="Planned"]

  9. Redemption

    A proposed route to request physical delivery or collection of the underlying metal, subject to thresholds, fees, compliance review and logistics that are not yet defined.

    [rc_status s="proposed" label="Planned · locked"]

Questions

Common questions about the journey

Today

Can I acquire tokens now?

No. No tokens are offered or sold through this website, and the token-acquisition module is inactive. Any message claiming otherwise should be treated as fraud. See the Anti-Fraud Notice.

Does creating an account or joining the waitlist reserve anything?

No. Neither creates a reservation, allocation, price reservation or priority of any kind.

Later

Will I be able to use any wallet?

The design allows delivery only to addresses approved in the ComplianceRegistry. Supported wallet types have not been decided.

Will tokens be freely transferable?

Transfers are designed to succeed only between approved, unfrozen addresses outside blocked jurisdictions. Whether any market will exist is not known, and none is promised.

Will redemption always be possible?

Redemption is a proposed framework. Thresholds, fees, logistics and eligibility are not defined, and redemption may not be possible in every jurisdiction. See How Redemption Works.
