Summary

Essential only, by default

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser. The default on reservechain.io is minimal. Public visitors receive at most one functional cookie, which remembers the language they chose. Authorised staff who sign in to the content-management system also receive WordPress login cookies. No analytics, advertising or social-media tracking cookie is set, and no such script is loaded, unless you have given consent.

Categories

Three categories, controlled separately

01

Essential

Needed for the website to work as you asked, or to keep it safe. Examples are remembering your language and keeping authorised staff signed in. These do not need consent. You can block them in your browser, but parts of the site may then not work.

[rc_status s="in_development" label="Active"]
02

Analytics

These would measure aggregate usage so the website can be improved. If they are introduced, they would use ReserveChain-controlled analytics, carry no personal data in URLs or events, and be set only after you opt in.

[rc_status s="not_applicable" label="None in use"]
03

Marketing

These would measure campaigns or personalise content. None are used. ReserveChain does not use third-party advertising trackers, and any future use would require your explicit opt-in.

Consent management

How consent will work

No consent banner is needed while only essential cookies are used. A consent tool will be in place before any analytics or marketing cookie is introduced.

  1. Separate choices

    Essential, analytics and marketing will be presented as separate controls. Only essential cookies will be on by default.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  2. Nothing before consent

    Non-essential scripts will not load until you opt in. Rejecting will be as easy as accepting.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  3. Change or withdraw at any time

    A persistent link in the footer will reopen your settings. Withdrawing consent stops further use and deletes the related first-party cookies.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  4. Consent recorded

    Your choice, its date and the policy version will be recorded, so the basis for any cookie can be shown later.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

Browser controls and changes

Managing cookies in your browser

Most browsers let you view, block and delete cookies. If you block rc_lang, the website will use your browser's language preference, or English. Blocking WordPress cookies prevents staff from signing in, but does not affect public pages.

Other technologies

The website does not use tracking pixels, fingerprinting scripts or third-party embeds that set cookies. Fonts and scripts are served from ReserveChain's own infrastructure. Browser storage, if used by interface components, holds only display preferences and never personal data.

Changes

This policy is versioned and fingerprinted in the Documentation Library. If a non-essential category is ever introduced, the policy will be updated and consent will be asked for before the category is activated.
