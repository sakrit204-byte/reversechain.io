Gallery status

An honest gallery starts empty

An image of copper powder in a dish shows only that someone owns copper powder. It does not show that it is this lot, in this box, at this purity. A ReserveChain gallery image counts as evidence only when it is a real, unedited photograph with its capture metadata and fingerprint, linked to a specific container passport.

The asset owner has not yet supplied and approved any photograph that meets that standard. The frames below show what will be published once photographs are supplied and approved.

Media usage-rights and publication policy

How an image becomes evidence

  1. Original capture supplied

    The unedited original file, with capture metadata kept. A cropped or retouched derivative may be published only alongside its original.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  2. Rights and source recorded

    The photographer or source, the licence, permitted uses and any third-party rights, recorded in the media usage-rights field.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  3. Fingerprinted and linked

    A SHA-256 fingerprint is computed and the file is linked to a specific lot or container passport.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  4. Captioned and described

    A factual caption and alternative text are written. They make no claim the evidence does not support.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  5. Reviewed and approved

    Maker-checker review is completed before the image is published. Every change is logged in the tamper-evident audit trail.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

Concept illustrations may appear elsewhere on the site only to explain an idea. They carry a visible "Concept illustration, not product evidence" label and are never attached to a passport, registry record or gallery.
