Platform

What is ReserveChain?

ReserveChain is a project in development. It is building infrastructure to register industrial metals unit by unit, attach fingerprinted evidence to each unit, and, subject to final legal structuring and approval, represent registered units digitally. It is not an exchange, a fund or a token sale.

What stage is the project at?

Pre-launch. The website, registry, Digital Asset Passports, audit trail and waitlist are live. Smart contracts exist as tested source code for test networks. Custody, Proof of Reserves, token issuance, purchase and redemption are not active. See Corporate Development Status.

Who operates ReserveChain?

A Swiss corporate and issuance structure is in development. No operating entity is asserted on this website until it has been incorporated. The Legal & Disclosures page will be updated when it is.

Can I acquire anything through this website today?

No. Nothing is offered or sold through this website. You can register interest on the waitlist. Registering creates no entitlement, allocation or price reservation.

Assets

Which assets are in scope?

Two initial industrial-metal programs: Ultra-High-Purity Copper Powder and High-Purity Nickel Wire 0.025 mm. Other categories, such as precious metals, gemstones, energy, real estate, and art and collectibles, are future, pre-launch concepts only.

Why industrial metals rather than gold?

Specialised industrial materials are defined by lot-specific assay, form and packaging. That makes them a demanding test of unit-level evidence, which is the core of the platform's design.

Are quantities and values published?

Declared quantities from owner-supplied certificates are shown and labelled as declared. No valuation is published. Any figure that has not been verified carries a pending status.

Can I submit my own asset?

Asset owners and originators can make an enquiry through Asset Owners & Originators. Onboarding requires due diligence and evidence. It does not imply acceptance.

Copper Powder

What purity is stated for the copper powder?

99.9999 % (6N). This is stated on IGAS Certificate of Analysis No. 0004512, dated 04.07.2022, for Lot #03-K-07, analysed by ICP/OES. The certificate is owner-supplied and subject to documentary verification.

How much material does the certificate cover?

The certificate records 2000 kg as declared by the customer. The quantity is a declaration on the certificate. It has not been independently weighed or confirmed.

How is it packaged?

According to the owner, in glass ampoules packed in cardboard boxes. Individual boxes are registered as containers. For example, Box no. 20 has passport RC-CU-CTN-000001.

Is the powder radioactive?

The certificate states natural isotopic composition and "not radioactive". That is the laboratory's statement on an owner-supplied copy. ReserveChain has not independently tested it.

Nickel Wire

What is the nickel wire's specification?

Nickel wire, 0.025 mm diameter, described on the certificate as "DKRNT NP1", with a stated purity of 99.9807 %. Source: IGAS Certificate of Analysis No. 0004368, dated 19.10.2021, Lot 120/NP1, analysed by ICP/MS and ICP/OES (owner-supplied).

How much wire is declared?

5000 g, declared as 30 bobbins in one box. Each bobbin has its own passport, from RC-NI-COL-000001 to RC-NI-COL-000030. The quantity is declared and has not been independently confirmed.

What does "impurities 0,0193 %" mean?

The certificate reports total impurities of 0,0193 % with reference to GOST 2179-75. That is consistent with the stated purity of 99.9807 %. The full element-by-element grid is transcribed on the program page.

Were all bobbins sampled?

No. According to the owner-supplied records, bobbins 8, 10, 19 and 27 were sampled. Their passports show this. The other bobbins' status reflects that they were not sampled. See bobbin 8.

Verification

Has any asset been independently verified?

No. Nothing is labelled as verified. The two certificates are owner-supplied copies and have not yet been confirmed with the issuing laboratory. The proposed independent verification framework is described on the verification page.

How can I check that a document is genuine?

Use the verification tool. Your browser computes the file's SHA-256 fingerprint locally and compares it with the registry. A match proves the copy is unaltered. It does not prove that the content is accurate.

What would independent verification involve?

Under the proposed framework: sampling under documented procedures, analysis by a laboratory selected by ReserveChain, validation of certificates with their issuers, and periodic re-verification. All of it is subject to final arrangements.

Custody

Where is the metal stored?

No custodian has been appointed, and no custody arrangement is represented. Vault locations will be published only once agreements are in place and evidenced.

Will metal be segregated?

The proposed model is segregated storage of registered units, with a custody record on each passport. This is a proposed framework subject to final contractual arrangements.

Is the metal covered by insurance?

No insurance arrangement exists or is represented. A proposed insurance framework is described in the whitepaper and is subject to final arrangements.

Proof of Reserves

Is Proof of Reserves live?

No. The module is inactive, no attestor has been appointed and no reserve attestation has been published. The dashboard shows declared and verified quantities separately. It never computes coverage that has not been evidenced.

How would it work?

Four proposed elements: a unit-level registry, an independent attestor reporting on registered units, the report's fingerprint posted on-chain to the ReserveGuard contract, and public reconciliation of attested units against tokens outstanding.

Does an on-chain attestation prove the metal exists?

No. It records a statement by a key holder at a point in time. How reliable it is depends on the attestor's independence and procedures, and on the custodian.

Digital Asset Passports

What is a Digital Asset Passport?

A public page for a single registered unit, such as a lot, box or bobbin. It shows the unit's identity, each factual field with its own status, a timeline, an evidence ledger with fingerprints and a Merkle root that commits to the evidence set.

Does a passport prove ownership?

No. A passport is an evidence record. It does not convey title or any legal right.

Which passports exist today?

Passports exist for the copper lot (RC-CU-LOT-000001), copper Box no. 20, the nickel lot (RC-NI-LOT-000001) and its 30 bobbins. RC-LOT-000001 is an illustrative template and is marked as such. Browse them in the registry.

Can a passport change?

Yes, through new evidence approved under four-eyes review. Every change is recorded in the hash-chained audit trail, so the history stays inspectable.

Tokenization

Has a token been issued?

No. No token has been issued on any production blockchain. Contracts have been tested for test networks only. Any testnet tokens have no economic value.

What is the token's name, symbol or price?

None has been decided. Name, symbol, ratio, supply cap and pricing methodology are all to be determined, subject to written approval. Treat any symbol or price circulating elsewhere as unofficial.

How would minting be controlled?

Under the proposed design, minting is reserve-gated and fail-closed. The ceiling is zero unless a fresh independent attestation exists and every parameter has been deliberately configured. Transfers would also pass an on-chain eligibility check.

Early Participation

What is the Early Participation Program?

A proposed future program for eligible, approved participants. It is not active. Token issuance remains inactive until the program is formally launched under definitive documentation.

What does joining the waitlist give me?

Updates only. It is not an investment, purchase, reservation, allocation or entitlement. You confirm by double opt-in, and the exact disclosure version you accepted is recorded.

What is the "20% discount methodology"?

A proposed methodology that is subject to approval. It is not a promotional offer. Its basis, conditions and exclusions are set out on the methodology page.

Can EU/EEA residents participate?

ReserveChain does not currently intend to offer tokens to residents of, or persons located in, the EU/EEA. EU/EEA registrations are recorded as restricted.

KYC

Will KYC be required?

Yes. Any future participation would require KYC for individuals, or KYB with beneficial owners for entities, together with AML risk assessment and sanctions and PEP screening.

Who will process identity documents?

A specialised KYC/KYB provider, still to be selected. The platform is designed to store only status values. Documents would stay with the provider.

Is personal data written on-chain?

No. The on-chain compliance registry is designed to hold only an address status, a jurisdiction code, a frozen flag and an expiry. It holds no names or documents.

Redemption

Can I redeem tokens for physical metal?

No. Redemption is a proposed feature. It is disabled on the platform and in the contract, and no redemption right exists.

How would redemption work if approved?

Tokens would be escrowed rather than burned. Compliance would review the request. On approval the tokens would be burned and the registered units released. On rejection the escrowed tokens would go back to the holder.

What would redemption cost?

That has not been determined. Thresholds, fees, delivery terms and eligible locations will be published only once approved.

Which modules could be licensed?

Do you work with industrial buyers?

Technology

Which blockchain will be used?

Test deployments are planned on Ethereum Sepolia, with Polygon Amoy as an option. No mainnet has been selected or deployed.

Has there been an independent audit of the smart contracts?

Not yet. They use established open-source libraries and automated tests. An independent smart-contract audit is planned before any mainnet consideration.

What is the audit trail?

An append-only log of material platform actions. Each entry includes the hash of the previous entry, so any alteration is detectable. Its head is intended to be anchored on-chain periodically.

Where are official contract addresses published?

Only on Official Smart-Contract Addresses. No production address exists.

Legal & Risk

Is ReserveChain regulated?

No regulatory authorisation has been obtained or is claimed. How any future token is classified depends on its final structure and on the jurisdiction, and is subject to legal analysis.

Is any token sale or pre-sale allocation taking place?

No. There is no sale of any kind. Anyone offering ReserveChain tokens, allocations or discounts is not acting for ReserveChain. Report it through the Anti-Fraud Notice.

What are the main risks?

Development and entity-formation risk, regulatory classification, asset specification and authenticity, custody and counterparty, smart-contract and technology risk, and the absence of any market or redemption. See the Risk Disclosure.

Should I take advice?

Yes. Nothing on this website is legal, tax, investment or financial advice. Obtain independent professional advice before making any decision.
