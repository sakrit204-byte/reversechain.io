Why these two

One bulk powder, one precision wire. One framework.

The two programs are deliberately different. One is a declared 2000 kg lot of powder in fragile glass ampoules. The other is a declared 5000 g lot of wire on 30 discrete bobbins. If one evidence framework can identify, reconcile and redeem both, it can be extended to other materials.

  • Different units of control: the container for copper, the coil (bobbin) for nickel
  • Different laboratory methods: ICP/OES for copper, ICP/MS plus ICP/OES for nickel
  • Different purity standards: TU 1793-011-50316079-2004 for copper, GOST 2179-75 for nickel

Shared verification and custody framework

Same gates, different units

This is a proposed framework, subject to final legal, contractual, technical and operational confirmation. Both programs pass through the same gates. Only the unit being counted, sealed and released differs.

  1. Document authenticity and ownership

    Confirm each certificate with its issuer and establish chain of title for each lot.

    [rc_status s="pending"]

  2. Unit census

    Copper: count, label and weigh every box. Nickel: label and weigh all 30 bobbins.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  3. Independent re-assay and characterisation

    Copper: particle data. Nickel: diameter and mechanical data.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  4. Custody intake

    Warehouse receipt for each unit, segregated storage and a seal register.

    [rc_status s="pending"]

  5. Valuation and reserve eligibility

    Independent valuation, then eligibility marked on each unit's passport.

    [rc_status s="pending"]
