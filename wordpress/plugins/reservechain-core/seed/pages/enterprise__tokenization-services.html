Service thesis

Tokenization is the last step, not the first

Most of the work in representing a physical asset digitally happens before any token exists: identifying the unit, proving what it is, proving where it is and who controls it, and deciding who may hold a representation of it. The proposed service is built around that sequence. It describes a proposed framework subject to final legal, contractual, technical and operational confirmation.

Smart-contract suite

Six contracts, one control model

The contract suite is written against OpenZeppelin v5 and deployed to public testnets only. No mainnet deployment has been performed, and no contract address on this site is an official production address until it appears on Official Smart-Contract Addresses.

Token name, symbol, decimals, cap and ratio are constructor or configuration inputs. None is hard-coded.

ReserveTokenERC-20 with permit, pausing and role-based mint and burn; transfer hook calls the compliance registry
ComplianceRegistryPer-address status, jurisdiction, expiry and frozen flag; blocked-jurisdiction set from configuration
ReserveGuardAttestor posts (program, units, report hash, URI); maximum issuable amount derived from attested units
RedemptionManagerEscrow, compliance review, approve-and-burn or reject-and-return; disabled by default
TreasuryRole-controlled, with withdrawal limits and a timelock option
AuditAnchorAnchors the head of the off-chain audit chain on-chain

Rule engine and participant controls

Rules enforced in code, decided by people

Rules are configured by authorised roles and enforced automatically. Every change to a rule is logged.

01

Eligibility rules

Only approved, unfrozen, unexpired addresses outside blocked jurisdictions can send or receive.

02

Issuance ceilings

Total supply per program cannot exceed attested reserve units multiplied by the approved ratio.

03

Emergency controls

Pausing and account freezing by separate roles, intended to be held by a multisignature wallet.

04

Role separation

Admin, minter, burner, pauser, compliance and treasury roles are distinct; admin transfer is time-delayed.

05

Redemption rules

Minimum and maximum thresholds and compliance review before any burn, configurable per program.

06

Activation gates

Issuance, wallet and redemption functions require explicit authorisation and a separate deployment action.

APIs and reporting

Evidence that systems can read

  • Program and passport endpoints returning field-level values with their claim status
  • Document-verification endpoint: submit a SHA-256 hash, receive a match or no match
  • Authenticated account endpoints with bearer tokens and TOTP multi-factor authentication
  • Reserve views computed from records, showing declared and verified quantities separately
  • Audit-trail export with integrity verification
  • Holdings and transaction endpoints return "not enabled" until authorised
