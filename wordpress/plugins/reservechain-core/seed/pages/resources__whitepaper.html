Purpose

What the paper sets out to answer

Q1

Can a physical metal unit be represented faithfully?

The paper sets out the evidence model. Each lot, container and coil gets its own Digital Asset Passport, field-level claim status, fingerprinted evidence and a Merkle commitment.

Q2

What stops a representation outrunning the reserve?

It describes reserve-gated minting. The mint ceiling is zero unless an independent attestation is fresh and the configuration is complete. The design is fail-closed by default.

Q3

What is still missing?

The paper states plainly what has not yet been decided. That includes the corporate structure, custodian, insurer, attestor, holder rights and every economic parameter.

Chapter summaries

The framework in brief

Short summaries of the main chapters. Each describes a proposed framework, subject to final legal, contractual, technical and operational confirmation.

Architecture

Four layers. A CMS-backed registry is the single source of truth. Public Digital Asset Passports and a REST API read from it. A tamper-evident audit trail records every material action. Test-network smart contracts mirror eligibility and reserve state without holding personal data.

Platform Infrastructure →

Asset framework

Programs contain lots, lots contain containers or coils, and each unit has its own passport. The initial programs are Ultra-High-Purity Copper Powder, 99.9999 %, and High-Purity Nickel Wire 0.025 mm, 99.9807 %. Both rest on owner-supplied IGAS certificates.

View Initial Programs →

Verification

Laboratory evidence, sampling records and certificate validation are fingerprinted and attached to the units they cover. Each factual field carries its own status, so a passport can be partly evidenced without overstating the whole.

View Verification Process →

Custody

The intended model is segregated storage of registered units with a professional custodian. Receipt, transfer and release would be evidenced by custody records on each passport. No custodian has been appointed.

Explore Custody →

Reserves

An attestor, not yet appointed, would report on registered units. Each report is fingerprinted, registered and posted to the ReserveGuard contract. The mint ceiling comes from the latest fresh attestation. The module is inactive.

Proof of Reserves →

Tokenization

An ERC-20 representation, if it is ever authorised. Every transfer passes an on-chain eligibility check, and minting is bounded by reserve attestations. Contracts exist as tested source code for test networks only. No tokenomics have been decided.

Learn About Tokenization →

Compliance

KYC for individuals and KYB for entities, together with AML risk assessment and sanctions and PEP screening. Jurisdictional eligibility is enforced on-chain through a registry that stores status flags, never documents. EU/EEA persons are not intended to be eligible.

Check Eligibility →

Enterprise model

The registry, passport engine, verification tooling and contract suite are designed as reusable modules. Third-party asset owners and platforms could license them, subject to approval.

Enterprise Services →

Roadmap, risk and governance

The roadmap has six phases, from Foundation to Controlled Activation, and none is dated. The risk chapter covers seven risk families. Governance rests on four-eyes approval, separation of duties and multisig administration.
