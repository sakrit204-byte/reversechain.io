Scope, not inventory

Designed to extend. Not yet extended.

The registry, passports and reserve engine are built around abstract units: a lot, a container, a coil. The same model could, in principle, describe a graded gemstone or a property title. This page explains how. It does not announce any program.

Six categories

What evidence each would require

Each class has its own equivalent of the Certificate of Analysis, its own unit of control and its own hardest verification problem. A category opens only when those are answered.

[rc_status s="proposed" label="Future Category / Pre-Launch"]

Precious Metals

Bars and grains with assay and refiner documentation.

  • Evidence: assay certificate, bar list
  • Unit: bar or sealed lot
  • Key issue: refiner provenance
[rc_status s="proposed" label="Future Category / Pre-Launch"]

Gemstones

Individually graded stones with no interchangeable units.

  • Evidence: grading report per stone
  • Unit: single stone
  • Key issue: identity matching
[rc_status s="proposed" label="Future Category / Pre-Launch"]

Energy Assets

Documented physical energy inventories and related assets.

  • Evidence: inventory and quality reports
  • Unit: tank, lot or asset record
  • Key issue: continuous measurement
[rc_status s="proposed" label="Future Category / Pre-Launch"]

Real Estate

Property interests, where a public title register replaces the laboratory.

  • Evidence: title extract, valuation
  • Unit: property or interest
  • Key issue: legal linkage of a token to title
[rc_status s="proposed" label="Future Category / Pre-Launch"]

Art & Collectibles

Unique objects whose value rests on provenance and condition.

  • Evidence: provenance file, condition report
  • Unit: single object
  • Key issue: authenticity opinion
[rc_status s="proposed" label="Future Category / Pre-Launch"]

Other Assets

Further classes, considered case by case through the asset-owner pathway.

  • Evidence: defined at intake
  • Unit: defined at intake
  • Key issue: whether a reliable evidence standard exists

Activation gates

What must happen before a category opens

These gates are proposed and subject to final legal and governance approval. Until every gate is passed, a category stays on this page and off every other.

  1. Legal and jurisdictional review

    Classification of the asset class and of any token linked to it, plus restrictions and eligibility.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  2. Evidence standard defined

    Which document proves identity, quality and quantity, and who may issue it.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  3. Custody and valuation model

    A credible holding arrangement and an independent valuation method for the class.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  4. Registry schema extension

    New record types and fields, with passports tested on illustrative data first.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  5. Governance approval

    Formal approval to move the category from pre-launch to an active program.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

Separation rule

Never mixed into current figures

  • No future category contributes to any registry count, reserve figure or program total.
  • No future category has a passport, lot or token series.
  • Every mention of a future category anywhere on the site carries the pre-launch badge.
  • No imagery is used that could be read as an existing holding.

Where the evidence is today

The current programs

Only two programs have documents behind them: ultra-high-purity copper powder (CoA 0004512) and ultrafine nickel wire (CoA 0004368). Both certificates are owner-supplied. Every future category would have to reach at least that level of evidence before it is admitted, and then go further.

* Owner-supplied purity, subject to documentary verification.
