Before you write

  • Never include passwords, private keys, seed phrases or payment details.
  • Do not send identity documents. KYC is not collected through this form.
  • For asset submissions, describe documents; we will request copies through an approved channel if relevant.
  • Submitting a message does not register you for the waitlist and does not create any entitlement.

This form is the official contact route while email addresses are being confirmed. See the Official Channels Directory.

What happens next

Handled under the same controls as the registry

01

Recorded

Your message is stored with its topic and consent record, and filtered for automated spam.

02

Routed

The topic determines which role reviews it. Security reports are handled separately from general correspondence.

03

Answered on-domain

Replies will come from a channel listed in the Official Channels Directory. Treat anything else as unverified.

Limits

No prices

No token price, valuation or discount has been approved.

No EU/EEA offers

ReserveChain does not currently intend to offer tokens to residents of, or persons located in, the EU/EEA.
