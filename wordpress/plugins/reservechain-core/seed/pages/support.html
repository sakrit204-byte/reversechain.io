Security and fraud reporting

Report first, then disengage

If someone claims to represent ReserveChain and asks for money, a wallet connection, a private key or a "reservation fee", stop contact and report it. No ReserveChain channel requests any of these.

Anti-Fraud NoticeOfficial channels
  • Impersonation or fake token sale: select topic "Security disclosure"; include the URL, handle or address and a screenshot.
  • Vulnerability in our website or apps: select "Security disclosure"; describe impact and steps to reproduce. Do not access other users' data.
  • Suspicious document: check its fingerprint on the verification page first; report if it claims to be ours and is not in the registry.

FAQ routing

Quick answers, with the right next page

Access

How do I get a portal account?

Accounts are provided in staged access during pre-launch. Join the waitlist to receive development updates and future eligibility information.

Can I connect a wallet?

No. Wallet connection is built but inactive and is not available on the public website. Any site asking you to connect a wallet for ReserveChain is not ours.

Programs and documents

Are the copper and nickel certificates independently verified?

No. They are owner-supplied certificates, transcribed exactly and fingerprinted, and remain subject to documentary verification and independent assessment. See Verification.

Where is the metal stored?

Custody arrangements have not been confirmed. Storage location fields show as pending in each registry record.

Tokens

Has a token been issued, or is there a contract address?

No token has been issued and no address is official. See Official Smart-Contract Addresses.
