Scope of this page

The custody model, before the custodian

This page describes a proposed custody framework, subject to final legal, contractual, technical and operational confirmation. No custodian, warehouse, vault or insurer has been appointed. No unit described on this site is held in professional custody under this framework.

We publish the model before any appointment so that prospective custodians, asset owners and participants can judge the control standard ReserveChain intends to require.

Custody model

Allocated, segregated, unit-addressable

The proposed model is allocated custody. Each physical unit is held in its own identity and recorded at the smallest level that can be redeemed: the container for copper powder and the bobbin for nickel wire.

01

Segregation

Program assets are held apart from the custodian's own stock and from other clients' stock. Each program is also segregated from the other. A unit is never pooled or swapped for a "similar" one.

02

Unit-level identity

Each container and bobbin carries a label that matches its registry identifier and passport. Copper Box no. 20, for example, is recorded as RC-CU-CTN-000001.

03

Material-appropriate storage

The conditions are set for each material. Sealed ampoules are protected against breakage and oxidation exposure. Fine wire is protected against deformation, humidity and handling damage. Specific requirements are pending documentation.

Chain of custody

Every movement leaves a record

  1. Inbound

    The custodian receives the units against an expected-receipt record. Seals are checked against the inspection record, then the units are weighed and photographed.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  2. Receipt

    The custodian issues a warehouse receipt for each unit. The receipt is uploaded, fingerprinted and linked to the passport.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  3. Holding

    The unit is stored in its assigned location. Any movement within the facility is logged with the time, the person and the reason.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  4. Inspection

    Scheduled counts and seal checks run on a sample of units, with a full count at least once a year. Ad hoc inspections can be called by the platform or an attestor.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

  5. Release

    Only against a release order that a second authorized person has approved and that refers to a completed burn on-chain.

    [rc_status s="proposed"]

Access controls and inventory records

Two keys for every door, one record for every unit

Access controls

  • Dual control for physical access to program stock
  • No ReserveChain staff member can remove a unit alone
  • Visitor and access logs supplied to the platform
  • Custodian staff screened under the custody agreement
  • Release instructions only through the authenticated workflow, never by email or phone

Inventory records

  • Unit identifier, seal number, gross and net weight
  • Location reference, published at the level approved
  • Receipt number and date
  • Inspection history with outcomes
  • Encumbrance and restriction status
  • Release history, linked to redemption records

Audit, reconciliation and insurance

Independent of the custodian's own word

A custodian's statement is evidence, but on its own it is not proof. The proposed framework compares three independent records and treats any difference as an exception.

Release and redemption controls

Burn first. Release second.

Physical release is the point where a digital record and a physical asset part ways. The order of operations is therefore fixed.

  • The holder's tokens are escrowed in the RedemptionManager contract.
  • Compliance approves the request and the specific container or bobbin to be released.
  • The tokens are burned on-chain, and the burn transaction is recorded against the request.
  • Only then does the custodian receive a dual-approved release order that cites the burn.
  • The passport moves to "Released", and the reserve figures update after reconciliation.

Follow custody into the reserve report

Custody records feed the Proof-of-Reserves dashboard. Until a custodian is appointed, the dashboard reports custody as pending.
