How eligibility works

Every check must pass, every time

Eligibility is the product of several independent checks. A single failed or expired check blocks access. ReserveChain does not verify identities itself: outcomes will come from an external KYC/KYB and screening provider, which has not yet been selected. Token issuance remains inactive until formally launched, so no check below grants any right today.

IND

Individuals

  • Adult with full legal capacity
  • Resident in, and located in, a permitted jurisdiction
  • KYC approved (identity, address, liveness)
  • AML, sanctions and PEP screening clear, or EDD satisfied
  • Source of funds explained; source of wealth where required
  • Professional or qualified-participant status where the final structure requires it
Required checks: KYC · AML · Sanctions/PEP · KYB not applicable
ORG

Companies and institutions

  • Duly incorporated and in good standing
  • Registered in, and operating from, a permitted jurisdiction
  • KYB approved, including ultimate beneficial owners
  • Directors, controllers and authorised signatories screened
  • Ownership structure transparent; no unexplained layering
  • Institutional or professional classification where required
Required checks: KYB · AML · Sanctions/PEP · KYC not applicable at entity level

Approval states

Five states, shown identically everywhere

The same five states are used in the participant portal, the mobile app, the API and the compliance back office. Each check (KYC, KYB, AML, sanctions/PEP) carries its own state.

Not started[rc_status s="not_applicable" label="Not started"]No submission has been made for this check.
Pending[rc_status s="pending" label="Pending"]Submitted and under review by the provider or the compliance team.
Approved[rc_status s="eligible" label="Approved"]The check has passed. Approval may carry an expiry and can be withdrawn after re-screening.
Rejected[rc_status s="restricted" label="Rejected"]The check has failed. The reason may not always be disclosed where law prohibits it.
Not applicable[rc_status s="not_applicable"]The check does not apply to this account type (for example, KYB for an individual).

Overall eligibility is shown as restricted, incomplete or eligible, subject to final approval. Even the last state does not confer any right to participate in any future offering.

Access restrictions

What eligibility controls

  • Restricted accounts can view public information and receive general updates only.
  • Program documents and the token-acquisition module would be visible only to approved accounts.
  • Tokens, if ever issued, could only move to addresses approved in the on-chain ComplianceRegistry.
  • Approvals can expire; an expired approval blocks transfers until renewed.
  • An account can be frozen on new sanctions or AML information, on-chain and off-chain at once.

Anti-nominee controls

No participation on someone else's behalf

  • Declaration that the participant acts for their own account
  • Funds must come from an account in the participant's own name
  • Wallet ownership checks before an address is approved
  • Detection of shared devices, addresses and payment sources across accounts
  • Related-account limits aggregated for program caps
  • Rejection and possible reporting where bypass is suspected

Record-keeping

Decisions you can trace and test

01

Outcome, not identity, on-chain

The ComplianceRegistry stores only status, jurisdiction code, expiry and a frozen flag per address. No name, document or personal data is written to a blockchain.

02

Tamper-evident audit trail

Every status change records who made it, when, the previous and new value and its source. Rows are hash-chained and cannot be edited or deleted through the admin.

03

Retention and privacy

Identity documents would be held by the provider and the issuer under retention periods set by applicable AML law, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Eligibility questions

Can I start KYC now?

No. The KYC/KYB module is built but inactive, and no provider has been selected. ReserveChain will never ask for identity documents by email, chat or social media.

Does passing KYC mean I can participate?

No. KYC is one of several conditions. Participation also requires an eligible jurisdiction, cleared screening, approved documentation and an activated program.

I live outside the EU/EEA but hold an EU passport. Am I eligible?

Residence, current location and, where legally required, nationality may all be relevant. The final rules are subject to legal review and will be set out in the definitive documents.
