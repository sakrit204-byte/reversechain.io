## 0. Top-line takeaways

1. **There are two page lists, and both apply.** WEB defines **51 numbered pages** (WEB p.4–21). MASTER §6 lists **16 main-nav items** and **33 "Required Page Structures"** (MASTER p.5). These overlap but are not identical. MASTER adds Cookie Policy, Official Channels Directory, Roadmap, Proposed Legal & Asset-Holding Structure, Individual Metal Lot/Batch page, Digital Asset Passport page, Project Overview, Custom 404 and General error page. The merged sitemap in §1.3 below contains **63 routes**.
2. **The homepage has 22 mandated sections in a fixed order** (MASTER p.5–6). The approved hero copy is fixed: *"Building the Infrastructure for Tokenized Industrial Metals"* (MASTER p.6). The WEB title, *"Tokenizing the World's Real-World Assets"* (WEB p.4), conflicts with it. MASTER is the binding scope, so its hero wins.
3. **The mandatory browser title is** `ReserveChain.io | Building the Infrastructure for Industrial-Metals Tokenization` (MASTER p.3).
4. **The asset programs are named and their purities are given:** "Ultra-High-Purity Copper Powder – 99.9999%" and "High-Purity Nickel Wire – 99.9807%" (MASTER p.3). WEB calls them "Ultrafine Copper Powder" and "Ultrafine Nickel Wire 0.025 mm". IGAS certificates are supplied for both, so lab name, CoA numbers, lot IDs and purity **are owner-supplied data** and are not invented.
5. **Estonia does not appear anywhere in either PDF.** Only a *Swiss* corporate and issuance structure "in development" is mentioned (MASTER p.1, 2, 6, 10, 23, 37, 55). The reference mockup shows "Headquarters (In Formation) ReserveChain AG, Baarerstrasse 25, 6300 Zug, Switzerland". That is unverified mockup data and must not be published.
6. **Brand:** deep navy/black, warm gold and restrained metallic accents. Use copper accents for Cu and nickel/silver/metallic-grey accents for Ni. Pair a premium serif or technical-display face for headings with a sans-serif for body text (WEB p.2–3, MASTER p.3). The logo is a gold-and-black hexagonal cube with the wordmark "ReserveChain.io" (navy "Reserve" + gold "Chain.io"). Its tagline is "THE INFRASTRUCTURE FOR REAL-WORLD ASSETS." with the motto "OWN. | TRADE. | REDEEM.".
7. **The reference mockups contain many prohibited elements.** These include invented reserve figures, fake partner logos (SGS, Intertek, eurofins, ALS), fake board members, "Verified/Secured/Insured" badges, "$RSC" token pricing, "Global Liquidity" and "100% backed" claims. The mockup's re-rendered nickel CoA also has **wrong values**. Use the mockups only for layout and density, never for data (see §3.5).
